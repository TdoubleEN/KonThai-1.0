# Selective Thai guidance

Read only the relevant item. These are editing options, not bans or proof of AI authorship. The brief, source and supplied voice take precedence.

- Structure: for a requested rewrite, front a topic or condition only if it resolves actual reading friction. Keep a clear original order, especially for proofreading. Preserve timing, causality and emphasis; neither subject-first nor condition-first is inherently wrong.
- Ownership: omit repeated subjects only when the referent is recoverable. With multiple actors/products, make each action, number and condition's owner explicit. If A stops when valve A closes while B continues, keep B's continuation tied to that same event; do not leave it sounding unconditional.
- Person roles: keep speaker, reader and third party consistent. Reports about เจ้าของร้าน may retain that noun; direct-address copy may use คุณ or natural omission. Do not convert a third-party observation into the reader's experience or a paraphrase into a testimonial.
- Register: retain brand mood and approved hook/CTA in marketing; show relationships and necessary terms in explainers; follow supplied pronouns/politeness in personal or spoken work; keep decisions, responsibilities and pending approvals explicit in formal/executive copy. Do not force slang, particles or a gendered voice.
- Rhythm: use ก็/ด้วย/แล้ว only when the intended relationship needs them. Do not imply completion or certainty absent from source. Reduce translated syntax and redundant connectors without losing a logical link. A useful question, summary, list or passive construction may stay.
- Wording: remove empty importance and repeated ideas only when no distinct fact or deliberate rhythm is lost. Do not invent taste, experience, measurements or benefits to replace vague promotional copy. Keep technical names consistent rather than varying synonyms for decoration.

When the text already works, keep it. A preference shown by one caption does not become a permanent rule.
